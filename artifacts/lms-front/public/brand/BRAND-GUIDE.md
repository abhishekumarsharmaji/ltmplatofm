# CoreSkils Brand Guide

## Brand name

Always write the name as **CoreSkils** (capital C and S, one “l”).

Official domain: **coreskils.org**

## Logo concept

The four-part core represents a strong foundation. The open upper-right space and rising arrow represent practical learning, progress and career growth.

## Colors

- Core green: `#10A364`
- Bright action green: `#15CF74`
- Ink: `#101614`
- Slate: `#42504A`
- Soft mint: `#E2FFF1`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

## Typography

Use **Outfit** for product UI and marketing. Use weights 400–700.

## Logo usage

- Use `logo-horizontal.svg` on light backgrounds.
- Use `logo-horizontal-white.svg` on dark backgrounds.
- Use `logo-mark.svg` where horizontal space is limited.
- Use `favicon.svg` or the PNG app icons for browser/app icons.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least one small square inside the mark.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor individual pieces or add effects to the mark.
