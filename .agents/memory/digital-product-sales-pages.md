---
name: Digital product sales pages
description: Durable rules for creator-authored digital product sales content and public rendering.
---

Store professional digital-product sales content as structured fields rather than arbitrary HTML. Sanitize and bound every string and collection on the server, and omit empty sections on the public page.

**Why:** Creators need flexible long-form sales pages, but arbitrary HTML creates security and consistency risks. Structured content also keeps old and partially completed products renderable.

**How to apply:** Extend the structured sales-page contract when adding new content types. Keep actual files, ownership, acquisition, and price authoritative in their existing systems; creator copy must never fabricate those facts.
