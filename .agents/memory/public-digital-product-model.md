---
name: Public digital-product model
description: Rules for keeping CoreSkils public commercial positioning genuine and consistent.
---

CoreSkils publicly presents a focused digital-products storefront. Product pages must state exact deliverables, price, licence, electronic delivery, refund conditions, support, and current payment availability. Old courses and test products remain private/redirected rather than being exposed publicly.

**Why:** Payment-provider reviews and customers need one consistent business model. Unsupported subscription plans, free-only claims, or an active-looking checkout before payment verification create contradictions and trust risk.

**How to apply:** Keep Products, Contact, delivery, refund, privacy, and terms easy to find. Do not reintroduce public course, creator-marketplace, test-product, or unsupported subscription claims. Do not invent reviews, sales counts, credentials, guarantees, or operational payment features. Until verified checkout exists, label paid products as payment activation in progress and collect no money.
